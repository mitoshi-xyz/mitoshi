### Is Mitoshi custodial?

No. The keys come from 24 words made in your browser and stay there, encrypted. Mitoshi's servers are a relay and a
gateway that pass sealed requests; there is no database and no account.

### Why a new wallet for every trade?

On a public venue a wallet is a track record. Copy-trading bots follow winning addresses, mirror entries and crowd
exits. A new wallet is usually found again through the money that funds it. Mitoshi funds each one from the shielded
pool through NEAR Intents, at a standard size and a random moment, so the trail stops at the pool.

### What can someone watching the venue see?

That wallet's own trades, its one funding from a NEAR swap and its one trip home. Not your other wallets, your balance
or your IP. See [What stays public](/privacy#what-stays-public).

### Does Mitoshi take a fee?

No fee on trades. A 0.2% funding fee exists in the code but is switched off. You pay the venues' own fees, the swap
spread and the Zcash network fee. See [Fees and timing](/fees-and-timing).

### Why does funding take minutes?

The swap through NEAR Intents takes about 7.5 minutes, and the funding starts at a random moment 20 seconds to 3
minutes after you ask. Turn on **Keep one ready** to have a funded wallet waiting.

### Why does my ZEC have to rest?

ZEC that arrives from a public address, including NEAR's payouts when a wallet comes home, rests 2–6 hours before it
can fund a wallet. Otherwise its arrival could be matched to your next trade. A deposit from a shielded wallet does
not rest.

### Can I use it on testnet?

The app runs on Zcash testnet locally (`pnpm --filter @mitoshi/web dev`), but private wallets need mainnet ZEC:
NEAR Intents only takes mainnet ZEC.

### Has a full mainnet trade run yet?

Not yet. The browser wallet's sends are verified on testnet, and each venue's reads and quotes over OHTTP are verified;
the full round trip on mainnet waits for mainnet ZEC in the test wallet. See [Verify it yourself](/verify).

### Is there a phone app?

No. Mitoshi is a web app, built mobile-first, at [mitoshi.xyz](https://mitoshi.xyz).

### Where is the code?

[Architecture](/architecture) page for a map.
