Your money stays in Zcash's shielded pool. When you open a position, Mitoshi makes a **brand-new wallet** for it,
funds it from the pool through **NEAR Intents** at a standard amount and a random moment, and trades on the venue's
real market. When you close, the money goes home to a fresh shielded address.

Anyone watching the venue sees a wallet with one trade and no history, and nothing that links it to your other
wallets or to you.

## Your first trade

<Steps>
<Step>

**Make a private balance.** Open [mitoshi.xyz/wallet](https://mitoshi.xyz/wallet), write down 24 words and check
three of them. The wallet runs in your browser.

</Step>
<Step>

**Deposit ZEC** from Zodl or any shielded wallet to the address Mitoshi shows. A fully shielded deposit is usable once
it confirms.

</Step>
<Step>

**Open a market**, say NVIDIA on Polymarket Perps. Type $25, pick 5× leverage and a take-profit.

</Step>
<Step>

**A private wallet is funded.** A new key is made for this position and paid one standard amount (0.01, 0.02,
0.05, 0.1… ZEC) through NEAR Intents. 1Click estimates about 7.5 minutes.

</Step>
<Step>

**Trade.** The order goes to the venue's own book, signed by the new wallet's key.

</Step>
<Step>

**Look at it as a stranger would.** "What the public sees" shows the wallet's public record: one funding, one
position, no history, no links.

</Step>
<Step>

**Close and come home.** The proceeds swap back to ZEC at a fresh shielded address.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you can trade

| Venue | What | Funded with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Polymarket](/venues/polymarket) | Prediction markets | USDC, credited as pUSD |
| [Polymarket Perps](/venues/polymarket-perps) | US stocks, indices, gold, oil | pUSD |
| [Hyperliquid](/venues/hyperliquid) | Crypto perps | USDC |
| [Solana](/venues/solana) | Tokens, through Jupiter | SOL |

No account, no sign-up, no fee from Mitoshi on trades.

## Where things stand

The app is live at [mitoshi.xyz](https://mitoshi.xyz). The browser wallet has proved and broadcast shielded spends on
Zcash testnet, directly and over Oblivious HTTP, and every venue's live data runs through the relay. **The full
round trip on mainnet (fund, trade, close, come home) has not run yet**, so no live trade is claimed here.
[Verify it yourself](/verify) lists the evidence.
