## Fees

| What | Charged by | How much |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trading | Mitoshi | **Nothing.** No fee and no builder code on any order |
| Venue fees | Polymarket, Hyperliquid, Jupiter | Each venue's own fees, as on the venue |
| Funding swap | NEAR Intents solvers | The swap spread, shown before you fund |
| Zcash network fee | Zcash miners | The standard ZIP 317 fee, about 0.0001–0.00015 ZEC per payment |
| Polymarket bridge | Polymarket's bridge provider | Shown before you fund (about $0.02 on $60, measured 2026-10-22) |
| Hyperliquid activation | Hyperliquid | 1 USDC, once, for a brand-new account |
| Trip home in ZEC | NEAR Intents | A fixed 0.00032 ZEC to pay ZEC out, plus the swap spread |

<Callout title="Mitoshi's funding fee is off">
A 0.2% funding fee is built in through NEAR's app fees, but no fee recipient is set (`APP_FEE_RECIPIENT` is `null` in
`packages/rails/src/constants.ts`), so it charges nothing. The Fund screen always shows the fee line before you
confirm.
</Callout>

## Timing

| Step | Typical time | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A new wallet starts funding | 20 s to 3 min after you ask, at random | Mitoshi's scheduler |
| A keep-ready replacement starts | 90 min on average after the last one is used, at most 12 h | Mitoshi's scheduler |
| Funding: ZEC in, venue asset out | About 7.5 min | 1Click estimates, 452–465 s (2026-10-22) |
| Polymarket credit: USDC to pUSD | About 30 s | Polymarket bridge quote, 27 s |
| Trip home: venue asset to ZEC | About 2.5 min | 1Click estimates, 140–150 s |
| Rest after a public arrival | 2 to 6 h, sampled per note | Mitoshi's rest rules |
| No sync after a send | 1 to 10 min, at random | ZIP 318 timing rule |
| Private proof in the browser | About 15–20 s | Measured on testnet, single-threaded |
