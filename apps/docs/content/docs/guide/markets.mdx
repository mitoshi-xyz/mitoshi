| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The price card | BTC, ETH, SOL, NVDA or GOLD, with a live price, its 24 h change and **LONG** / **SHORT**, which open the terminal |
| **01 Predictions** | Six Polymarket events with live odds; **See all →** opens [Predict](/docs/guide/predict) |
| **02 Perpetuals** | Two tabs: **Crypto** (Hyperliquid) and **Stocks · gold · oil** (Polymarket Perps), with price, change, funding and open interest |
| **03 Solana tokens** | Eight trending tokens; **See all →** opens [Tokens](/docs/guide/tokens) |

## Getting around

| | Desktop (1280 px and up) | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Places | The rail: Markets, Perps, Predict, Tokens, Portfolio, Wallets (keys 1–6), More | The dock: Markets, Perps, the seal (your money), Predict, More |
| Your balance | The money plate at the top of the rail, with a **MAINNET** badge and an eye to hide amounts | Behind the seal in the dock |
| Search | **Search markets and pages** (⌘K or /) | The search icon |
