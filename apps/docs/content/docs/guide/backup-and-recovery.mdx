Mitoshi keeps nothing for you on a server, so recovery is yours.

<Screen name="settings" alt="Settings on mitoshi.xyz: Security with Recovery words, Backup file and Passphrase lock, then Find wallets from your words." caption="Settings: everything about your keys lives here." />

| Way back | Brings back | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **24 words** | Your shielded balance, and your Hyperliquid and Solana wallets | Slow: a private scan of the chain, then of those two venues |
| **Backup file** | Your words and your whole private-wallet list, Polymarket's included, with each wallet's funding record | The wallet list at once, with no venue scan; the balance still needs the private chain scan |

## The 24 words

The words restore the shielded balance with a private scan from the Ironwood upgrade (block 3,428,143). Then Mitoshi
looks for the private wallets they made on **Hyperliquid and Solana**, asking each venue about one derived address at a
time, 3–9 seconds apart, until it meets five unused ones in a row. **Settings → Find my wallets** runs that scan again.

<Callout type="warn" title="Polymarket wallets need the backup file">
The scan doesn't look for Polymarket and Polymarket Perps wallets. Their keys still come from your words, but only the
backup file puts them back on your list.
</Callout>

## The backup file

<Shot src="/shots/wide/backup.webp" alt="The Backup file section of Settings on mitoshi.xyz with two empty passphrase fields and Download the backup." caption="Make a backup file: a passphrase twice, then Download the backup." />

**Settings → Backup file → Make a backup file** asks for a passphrase of 8 characters or more and downloads
`mitoshi-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`: your words and your private-wallet list, encrypted with AES-GCM under a key from your
passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations). Restoring from it brings the whole wallet list back at once, with no venue scan; the shielded balance still comes
back through the private chain scan from Ironwood. There is no
reset: if the passphrase is lost, only your 24 words get you back in.

## The passphrase lock

**Settings → Passphrase lock** asks for a passphrase each time Mitoshi opens in this browser. The vault key is then kept
only wrapped under your passphrase; turning the lock on or off re-encrypts every record under a fresh key in one step.
A locked vault opens nothing, not even the wallet engine, until it is unlocked. **Lock now** locks it at once.

## Starting over

<Steps>
<Step>
**Settings → Forget this browser**, behind a typed confirmation (FORGET), erases the vault here. Your money stays on the
chains.
</Step>
<Step>
Restore from the 24 words or the backup file, in any browser: **I have a recovery phrase** on the first screen.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone with your 24 words can move your money. Keep them offline and never type them into a website. Settings can show
them (blurred until you hold), so don't open that with anyone watching.
</Callout>

## If something stops halfway

| What stopped | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A funding failed before anything was sent | It says so; **Try again** waits for a new random start |
| A funding's ZEC was sent | It goes back to following the swap; NEAR refunds to a fresh shielded address if it can't complete |
| NEAR refunded a trip home | The money is back in the venue wallet: send it home again on Hyperliquid and Solana; on Polymarket it is USDC on Polygon, which Send home doesn't pick up yet |
| The tab closed during a funding | Every quote is saved before any ZEC moves, so the swap is found again on the next open; a funding that came due while closed gets a new random start |
