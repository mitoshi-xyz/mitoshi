its leading outcomes and its odds. Open one for the event page.

## The event page

<Screen name="event" alt="A Polymarket event on mitoshi.xyz: its title, the chance, the one-month odds chart, the outcomes with Yes and No prices, and the ticket." caption="The event page: chance, odds history, every outcome, the order book and the rules." />

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Header | The title, **Ends …**, **$X traded**, **$X liquidity** |
| Chance | The leading outcome's chance, and an odds chart over **1H 6H 1D 1W 1M All** |
| Outcomes | The top four with **Yes** and **No** prices; **All N outcomes** opens the rest |
| Order book | Price, shares and total on each side, and the spread |
| Rules | The market's resolution rules and source |

## The ticket

  <Phone src="/shots/phone/event.webp" alt="The event on a phone with the pinned Yes and No buttons." caption="On a phone, Yes and No are pinned above the dock." />
  <Phone src="/shots/phone/event-ticket.webp" alt="The ticket sheet on a phone: Buy, Market, the outcome prices and the amount." caption="The ticket opens as a sheet with a keypad." />

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy / Sell** | Buy shares with pUSD, or sell shares you hold |
| **Market / Limit** | At market (fill-and-kill, within two ticks of the estimate) or at your price, 1¢ to 99¢ |
| **Amount** | Dollars to spend, with **$5 $10 $25 $100**; or shares to sell |
| **Shown** | **Cost**, **To win** and **Chance** for a buy; **Shares**, **You get** and **Chance** for a sell |
| **Button** | **Buy Yes** / **Sell No** with a wallet; **Fund a private wallet** without one |

Each market lives in its own wallet. The ticket names it (**Fresh wallet 0x12ab…cdef** or **Holding wallet …**), and
that link opens "What the public sees" for it. Open orders show under the ticket with **Cancel**; after resolution the
button becomes **Redeem N winning shares**.

How the wallet behind it is made, funded and sent home: [Polymarket](/docs/venues/polymarket).
