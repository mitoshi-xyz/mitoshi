A private wallet is a venue account made from a **new key** for **one position**, funded once and sent home once. No two
share an address, a key or a swap.

<Screen name="wallets" alt="The Wallets screen on mitoshi.xyz: New, Keep one ready with a switch for Polymarket, Hyperliquid and Solana (on for Hyperliquid at 0.05 ZEC), and the Live and Done tabs with no wallets yet." caption="Wallets, for a new account: Keep one ready, set per venue, and no private wallets yet." />

## What makes one

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Its own key** | From your 24 words on a hardened path of Mitoshi's own: `m/44'/coin'/6874'/venue'/index'` (coin 60 for Polymarket and Hyperliquid, 501 for Solana). 6874 spells MTSH on a phone keypad, so it never collides with a normal wallet's path |
| **One position** | An order goes to the wallet that already holds that market; otherwise to a ready wallet that has never traded; otherwise you fund a new one. Another position's wallet is never used |
| **A standard size** | 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 ZEC, so its funding hop looks like many others |
| **Only on this device** | The list of your wallets lives in the encrypted vault in your browser; no server holds it |

## Its life

<Lifecycle />

| Step | What happens | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preparing** | A key is derived; funding waits for a random moment | 20 s to 3 min ("Starts in about N min, so the start can't be matched to anything you did") |
| **Funding** | A signed 1Click quote, then one shielded payment of exactly the size | About 8 min by 1Click's estimate; Polymarket adds the bridge credit |
| **Ready** | Funded and unused | Until you trade |
| **Trading** | Orders signed by this wallet's key | Until you send it home |
| **Coming home** | The venue's positions closed or sold, the rest swapped to ZEC at a fresh shielded address | A few minutes, then the ZEC rests 2–6 h |
| **Retired** | Empty; never used again | — |

A funding that NEAR can't complete is **Refunded** to a fresh shielded address. A step that stopped shows **Needs
you**, with **Try again**; a funding that never sent anything waits for a new random start. Wallets that came home move
to the **Done** tab.

## Keep one ready

<Shot src="/shots/wide/keep-ready.webp" alt="Keep one ready on mitoshi.xyz with the Hyperliquid switch on, a 0.05 ZEC size chip and Waiting for ZEC." caption="Keep one ready, on for Hyperliquid at 0.05 ZEC. With no ZEC to spend, it waits." />

A trade waits for a funding unless a wallet is already ready. **Keep one ready** funds one wallet per venue, at the
size you pick, while Mitoshi is open and there is ZEC to spend. Turn the switch on with ZEC to spend and the first
wallet starts like one you asked for, 20 s to 3 min later. Every wallet after it, the replacement for one you used or
one made later when ZEC arrives, starts after a random wait from an exponential spread, **90 minutes on average and
never more than 12 hours**, so a new wallet never shows up on the venue minutes after your trade. The switch's line
says when the waiting wallet starts.

## Dust

Coming home costs a swap, so whatever is smaller than the trip is **left in the venue wallet as dust**, never moved into
another wallet:

| Venue | Left as dust |
| --- | --- |
| Polymarket, Polymarket Perps | Under 1 pUSD |
| Hyperliquid | Under 1 USDC |
| Solana | Under 0.005 SOL |

## What the public sees

Every wallet has a **What the public sees** view: its record as a copy trader would find it. When it was first seen,
its fills or transactions, its deposits and withdrawals and its open positions are read live from the venue over
Oblivious HTTP. The link checks are made on this device against your own wallet list: its own key and path, no transfer
to or from your other wallets, no shared deposit, refund or payout address, and one NEAR swap at a standard size. It
needs a funded wallet, so there's no picture of it here yet.
