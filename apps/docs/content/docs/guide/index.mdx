Your money stays in Zcash's shielded pool, in a real Zcash wallet that runs in your browser. When you open a position,
Mitoshi gives it a **brand-new wallet**, funds it through **NEAR Intents** with one standard amount at a random moment,
and trades on the venue's own market. When you're done, the money comes home to a fresh shielded address.

A copy trader watching the venue finds a wallet with one funding, one position and no history, and nothing that links
it to your other wallets or to you.

## Your first trade

<Steps>
<Step>

**Make a private balance.** Open [mitoshi.xyz/wallet](https://mitoshi.xyz/wallet), write down 24 words and check three
of them. No account, no sign-up. → [Getting started](/docs/guide/getting-started)

</Step>
<Step>

**Deposit ZEC** from Zodl or any shielded wallet to the fresh address Mitoshi shows. A fully shielded deposit is
usable once it confirms.

</Step>
<Step>

**Pick a market**, say NVIDIA on Polymarket Perps. The ticket starts with the amount: **$25 at 5×** is a **$125.00**
position, **0.58768 NVDA** at the price when this was captured, fee **$0.05**. → [Trade](/docs/guide/trade)

</Step>
<Step>

**Fund a private wallet.** With no ready wallet for that venue, the ticket says **Fund a private wallet**. Pick a
standard size (0.05 ZEC by default); Mitoshi derives a new key and, at a random moment 20 s to 3 min later, pays
exactly that much from the shielded pool into NEAR Intents. On 2026-10-23 a confidential quote for 0.05 ZEC gave
**≈ 60.79 USDC on Hyperliquid in about 8 minutes**. → [Private wallets](/docs/guide/private-wallets)

</Step>
<Step>

**Trade.** When the wallet is ready, place the order: it goes to the venue's real book, signed by that wallet's own
key (on Polymarket Perps, by a short-lived key that wallet authorised).

</Step>
<Step>

**See what a copy trader sees.** "What the public sees" reads the wallet's public record from the venue: one funding,
one position, no links to your other wallets.

</Step>
<Step>

**Come home.** Close the position, then **Send home**: the proceeds swap back to ZEC at a fresh shielded address.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Skip the wait">
Turn on **Keep one ready** for a venue and Mitoshi keeps a funded wallet waiting while the app is open, so the next
position doesn't wait for a funding.
</Callout>

## Where things stand

The app is live on Zcash mainnet at [mitoshi.xyz](https://mitoshi.xyz), with live data from every venue through the
relay. The browser wallet's proofs and sends are verified on testnet, and confidential NEAR quotes are verified over
Oblivious HTTP. **The full round trip on mainnet (fund, trade, close, come home) has not run yet**, so no live trade is
claimed here. [Verify it yourself](/docs/privacy/verify) lists the evidence.
