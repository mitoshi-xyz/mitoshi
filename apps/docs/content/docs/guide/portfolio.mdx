| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total balance** | One figure for everything you own, in dollars and live: your shielded ZEC at the live ZEC price plus every private wallet's cash, tokens and positions at their live prices, with today's change under it. Tap it for the breakdown: **Shielded ZEC**, **Cash on venues**, **Tokens**, **In positions**, each with its share, then **Today**, **All-time** and **Swap costs**. **Deposit**, **Withdraw** and **Wallet** (a new private wallet) sit under it |
| Sync | "Scanning privately · N blocks left", then "Synced · block N" |
| **Your assets** | Token by token, largest first: **ZEC** (shielded, with how much is spendable, resting or confirming), each venue's cash (**USDC** on Hyperliquid, **pUSD** on Polymarket, **SOL** on Solana) and each Solana token, with its amount in its own unit and its dollar value |
| **Positions** | Shown once you hold one: every open position in every wallet, moving with the market: market, size, entry, mark, P&L, with **What the public sees**, **Close** for perps and **Sell** or **Redeem** for the rest |
| **History** | Your Zcash transactions (tap one for its **Receipt**: status, time, block, network fee, where it came from, and the transaction id), and each wallet's fundings, trades and trips home |
| **Wallets** | Each private wallet's value, P&L and story |

Everything counts once: a wallet's cash is in **Your assets**, its positions are in **Positions**, and both are in the
total.

<Shot src="/shots/wide/portfolio-history.webp" alt="The History tab of Portfolio on mitoshi.xyz with No transactions yet." caption="History, before the first deposit." />

Positions and wallet values are read from each venue over Oblivious HTTP, each wallet on its own random rhythm, so the
gateway never sees your addresses asked about as one batch.
