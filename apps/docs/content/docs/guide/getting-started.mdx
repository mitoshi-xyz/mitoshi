There is no account. Everything starts from 24 words made in your browser.

## 1. Create the wallet

<Screen name="wallet" alt="The first screen of the wallet on mitoshi.xyz: Your money, shielded, with Create a private wallet, I have a recovery phrase and Look around first." caption="The first visit: a short story, then Create a private wallet." />

<Steps>
<Step>

Open [mitoshi.xyz/wallet](https://mitoshi.xyz/wallet) and choose **Create a private wallet**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Write down these 24 words.** They stay blurred until you press and hold **Hold to reveal**.

</Step>
<Step>

**Check three words**: pick the right word for three positions.

</Step>
<Step>

**Create my wallet.** Mitoshi saves your words encrypted in this browser, makes your shielded account and syncs with
Zcash. Then it opens your Portfolio.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot src="/shots/wide/onboarding-words.webp" alt="The Write down these 24 words step on mitoshi.xyz: the word grid blurred, the Hold to reveal button, the warning never to type them into a website, and I wrote them down." caption="Step 1 as it looks before you hold the button. For this picture the words were also replaced with dots in the page; Mitoshi itself only blurs them." />

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone with the 24 words can move your money. Keep them offline. Mitoshi never asks for them, and no website should.
</Callout>

Restoring instead? **I have a recovery phrase** takes your 24 words or a backup file. See
[Backup and recovery](/docs/guide/backup-and-recovery).

## 2. Back it up

| Option | What it holds | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **24 words** | The balance and every private wallet's key | On paper, offline |
| **Backup file** | Your words and your private-wallet list, locked with a passphrase | Settings → Backup file |
| **Passphrase lock** | Asks for a passphrase each time Mitoshi opens in this browser | Settings → Passphrase lock |

Make a backup file once you have private wallets: it is the only way to bring back Polymarket and Polymarket Perps
wallets on a new browser.

## 3. Deposit

<Screen name="deposit" alt="The Deposit ZEC drawer on mitoshi.xyz: a QR code with the Zcash mark and a fresh shielded address (both blurred here), Copy address, Open in wallet, Another fresh address, and Waiting for your deposit." caption="Deposit ZEC: a fresh shielded address and its QR code, every time you open it (blurred in this picture)." />

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Deposit** (on Portfolio, or in **More**). It makes a **fresh shielded address** each time: a unified address
with only an Orchard receiver, never reused.
</Step>
<Step>
In Zodl (or any shielded Zcash wallet), scan the QR code or paste the address and send ZEC.
</Step>
<Step>
It shows up once it's mined (mainnet blocks come about every 75 seconds; the wallet checks every 30). It can fund a
wallet after **10 confirmations**, about 12–13 minutes; until then Portfolio shows it under **Confirming**.
</Step>
</Steps>

What happens next depends on where the ZEC came from:

| Your deposit came from | It can fund a wallet |
| --- | --- |
| A shielded address (Zodl's default) | After 10 confirmations, about 12–13 minutes |
| An exchange (Deposit → **Exchange**: a fresh `t1…` address Mitoshi shields for you) | After resting 2–6 hours, picked per note |

Exchanges refuse the shielded address, which is why the **Exchange** tab exists ([how it works](/docs/guide/wallet#from-an-exchange)).
The rest keeps an exchange withdrawal from being matched to your next trade.

## 4. Open a market

Pick a market on [Markets](/docs/guide/markets), [Predict](/docs/guide/predict), [Trade](/docs/guide/trade) or
[Tokens](/docs/guide/tokens). Your first trade on a venue asks you to fund a
[private wallet](/docs/guide/private-wallets) for it, which takes a few minutes. **Keep one ready** funds one
ahead of time, so a later trade needn't wait.
