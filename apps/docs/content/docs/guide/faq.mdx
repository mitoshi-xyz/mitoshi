<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Is Mitoshi custodial?">
No. The keys come from 24 words made in your browser and stay there, encrypted. Mitoshi's servers are a relay and a
gateway that pass sealed requests; there is no database and no account.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why a new wallet for every trade?">
On a public venue a wallet is a track record. Copy-trading bots follow winning addresses, mirror entries and crowd
exits, and a new wallet is usually found again through the money that funds it. Mitoshi funds each one from the
shielded pool through NEAR Intents, at a standard size and a random moment, so the trail stops at the pool.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What can someone watching the venue see?">
That wallet's own trades, its one funding from a NEAR swap and its one trip home. Not your other wallets, your balance
or your IP. See [What stays public](/docs/privacy/what-stays-public).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does Mitoshi take a fee?">
No. Mitoshi takes no fee on fundings, trips home or orders. You pay NEAR Intents' 0.2% on each swap and
the spread (the funding sheet's **Swap cost**), the venues' own fees, and the Zcash network fee. A trip home also has a
fixed part, the same on any amount (from Hyperliquid, about $1.60), and the Send home sheet shows it before you send. See [Fees and timing](/docs/guide/fees-and-timing#the-trip-home).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why does funding take minutes?">
The swap through NEAR Intents takes about 8 minutes by its own estimate, and the funding starts at a random moment 20
seconds to 3 minutes after you ask. Turn on **Keep one ready** and Mitoshi funds one ahead of time while the app is
open; each one after the first waits a random 90 minutes on average, so it can't be matched to your last trade.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why does my ZEC have to rest?">
ZEC that arrives from a public address, including NEAR's payouts when a wallet comes home, rests 2–6 hours before it can
fund a wallet. Otherwise its arrival could be matched to your next trade. A deposit from a shielded wallet doesn't rest; it
only needs 10 confirmations, about 12–13 minutes.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I try it on testnet?">
The app runs on Zcash testnet locally (`pnpm --filter @mitoshi/web dev`), but private wallets need mainnet ZEC: NEAR
Intents only takes mainnet ZEC.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Has a full mainnet trade run yet?">
Not yet. The browser wallet's sends are verified on testnet, and Polymarket reads, a bridge quote and a NEAR quote
over Oblivious HTTP are verified; the full round trip on mainnet waits for test funds. See [Verify it yourself](/docs/privacy/verify).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is there a demo video?">
Not yet: [mitoshi.xyz/demo](https://mitoshi.xyz/demo) says "Recording soon" and links to the live screens.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is there a phone app?">
No. Mitoshi is a web app, built for phones first, at [mitoshi.xyz](https://mitoshi.xyz).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where is the code?">
[github.com/mitoshi-xyz/mitoshi](https://github.com/mitoshi-xyz/mitoshi), MIT licensed. Start with the
[code map](/docs/build/code-map).
</Accordion>
