There is no account. Everything starts from 24 words made in your browser.

## 1. Create the wallet

<Steps>
<Step>

Open [mitoshi.xyz/wallet](https://mitoshi.xyz/wallet) and choose **Create my wallet**.

</Step>
<Step>

Hold to reveal the **24 words** and write them down on paper. They show only while you hold.

</Step>
<Step>

**Check three words** to prove the copy is right.

</Step>
<Step>

Mitoshi saves the words encrypted in this browser, makes your shielded account and syncs with Zcash. The wallet
engine is Zcash's own `zcash_client_sqlite`, compiled to WebAssembly and run in a Web Worker.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone with the 24 words can move your money. Keep them offline. Mitoshi never asks for them, and no site should.
</Callout>

## 2. Back it up

| Option | What it holds | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **24 words** | Everything: the balance and every private wallet's key | Paper, offline |
| **Backup file** | Words and your private-wallet list, locked with a passphrase | Settings → Download the backup |
| **Passphrase lock** | Asks for a passphrase each time Mitoshi opens here | Settings → Passphrase lock |

The backup file restores your wallets at once. Words alone also work, with a slow private scan. See
[Backup and recovery](/backup-and-recovery).

## 3. Deposit from Zodl

<Steps>
<Step>

In Mitoshi, open **Deposit**. It makes a **fresh shielded address** each time: a unified address with only an Orchard receiver, so nothing about it is public.

</Step>
<Step>

In Zodl (or any shielded Zcash wallet), send ZEC to that address.

</Step>
<Step>

Wait for one block. Mainnet blocks come about every 75 seconds, and the wallet checks for new ones every 30 seconds.

</Step>
</Steps>

What happens next depends on where the ZEC came from:

| Your deposit came from | It can fund a wallet |
| --- | --- |
| A shielded address (Zodl's default) | Once it confirms |
| A public (transparent) address, such as an exchange | After resting 2–6 hours, picked at random per note |

The rest keeps an arrival from a public address from being matched to your next trade.

## 4. Open a market

Pick a market on [Predict](https://mitoshi.xyz/predict), [Trade](https://mitoshi.xyz/trade) or
[Tokens](https://mitoshi.xyz/tokens). The first trade on a venue funds a new
[private wallet](/private-wallets) for it; that takes a few minutes. To skip the wait next time, turn on **Keep one
ready** for that venue.
