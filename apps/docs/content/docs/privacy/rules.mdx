## Rules that keep wallets apart

| Rule | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A new key per wallet | `m/44'/coin'/6874'/venue'/index'`, every level hardened | No two wallets share an address or a key |
| One wallet per position | the wallet that holds the market, else a never-used ready one, else a new funding | Positions never share a wallet |
| Standard sizes only | 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 ZEC | The funding hop looks like many others |
| A random start | 20 s to 3 min after you ask | The hop is not timed to your tap |
| Keep-ready refills | the first, when you switch it on: like one you asked for; every one after: exponential, 90 min on average, at most 12 h | A new wallet never shows up right after your trade |
| Due while closed | a new random start when the app opens | The hop is not timed to the app opening |
| Rest after a public arrival | 2–6 h, picked per note | An arrival can't be matched to your next trade |
| Fresh addresses | a fresh shielded refund address for every funding, a fresh shielded payout address for every trip home | Swaps share no Zcash address |
| No memos | never, on any send; the outgoing viewing key is discarded | Nothing marks or reveals the payment |
| Quiet after a send | no sync for a random 1–10 min | A broadcast and the same wallet's reads never share a window |
| No builder code | on any order, on any venue | Orders don't name Mitoshi |

## The leak meter

<Screen name="fund" alt="The New private wallet sheet on mitoshi.xyz with an empty balance: the Hyperliquid tab, 0.05 ZEC chosen, a NEAR Intents quote, and the leak meter blocking the funding because no ZEC is ready." caption="With nothing to spend, the meter blocks the funding and says why; the funding button asks for a deposit first." />

Every send runs through it (`packages/engine/src/leak.ts`).

| Send | Red | Amber | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Funding a private wallet | not a standard size, or it would spend more than is ready (resting ZEC doesn't count) | — | Red **blocks** the funding |
| Withdrawal to a shielded address | — | — | Always clear: the amount and both addresses stay private |
| Withdrawal to a public address | exactly the amount that came home in the last 48 h | within 2% of it; within 2 h of a payout; not a standard size | Advisory: sending to your own exchange is your choice |

Sapling addresses (`zs1…`) are refused as a destination; Mitoshi asks for a unified address.
