| Venue | You trade | The wallet | Funded with | Key path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Polymarket](/docs/venues/polymarket) | Prediction markets: Yes/No and multi-outcome events | A Polymarket Deposit Wallet for a fresh signer key, one market per wallet | USDC, credited as pUSD by Polymarket's bridge | `m/44'/60'/6874'/0'/i'` |
| [Polymarket Perps](/docs/venues/polymarket-perps) | Perps on US stocks, indices, commodities and crypto | The same kind of fresh Polymarket wallet; a 7-day proxy key signs its orders | pUSD moved into Perps, at least 10 | `m/44'/60'/6874'/0'/i'` |
| [Hyperliquid](/docs/venues/hyperliquid) | Crypto perps | A fresh EVM key that signs its own orders | USDC on HyperCore | `m/44'/60'/6874'/1'/i'` |
| [Solana](/docs/venues/solana) | Any token Jupiter routes | A fresh ed25519 key (SLIP-0010) | SOL, which also pays the fees | `m/44'/501'/6874'/2'/i'` |

Every venue call goes through the [gateway's allowlist](/docs/privacy/oblivious-http), and every funding and trip
home goes through [NEAR Intents](/docs/venues/near-intents). No order carries a builder code.
