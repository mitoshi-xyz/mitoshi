## Set up

Polymarket's official TypeScript SDK runs in its own Web Worker whose only network is the sealed fetch. The Deposit
Wallet's address is known before it exists (it is derived from the signer), so Polymarket's bridge can give it a
deposit address before it is deployed; the wallet is deployed and approved **gasless** through Polymarket's relayer on its first trade. The builder secret that
authorises those relayer calls stays on the gateway, which [signs only Deposit Wallet calls](/docs/privacy/oblivious-http#the-builder-signer).

## Funding

<Steps>
<Step>
One standard amount of ZEC goes to a NEAR Intents deposit address, with a fresh shielded refund address.
</Step>
<Step>
NEAR delivers USDC to the wallet's **Polymarket bridge address**: on Polygon, or on Arbitrum or Base when NEAR has no
Polygon route at that moment.
</Step>
<Step>
The bridge credits **pUSD** to the Deposit Wallet. The wallet is ready when the bridge reports it complete.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Trading

  <Phone src="/shots/phone/event-ticket.webp" alt="The Polymarket ticket sheet on a phone: Buy, Market, the Yes and No prices, an amount on the keypad, and the button to fund a private wallet." caption="The ticket opens as a sheet with a keypad." />

- **Buy or sell** any outcome **at market** (fill-and-kill, within two ticks of the estimate) or with a **limit**
  (good till cancelled); cancel open orders; **redeem** winning shares after the market resolves.
- **One market per wallet.** Adding to a position or selling it stays on the wallet that holds it; a new market takes
  a ready wallet that has never traded, or asks you to fund one.
- **No builder code** on orders (`packages/venues/src/polymarket/account.ts`).

## Coming home

**Send home** cancels open orders, redeems resolved shares, sells the rest at market and brings any Perps money back
first. The pUSD then leaves through a one-time **bridge withdrawal** that pays a NEAR deposit address in USDC on
Polygon, and NEAR swaps it to ZEC at a fresh shielded address. Under 1 pUSD stays behind as dust. If NEAR refunds the
trip home, the money returns to the Deposit Wallet as USDC on Polygon; the next Send home sends it through the wallet's
own bridge address, back into pUSD, and out with the rest (the bridge takes $2 or more).

<Callout title="Not in NEAR's confidential mode">
This is the one swap Mitoshi can't make confidential: the bridge pays out an inexact amount, which needs a
flexible-input quote, and 1Click offers no confidential mode for those. NEAR's record pairs that payout with the fresh
shielded address it goes to.
</Callout>

## Public on purpose

Each wallet's **relayer calls** (creating it, its approvals, redeeming, moving pUSD into Perps, the trip home) are
attributed to Mitoshi's builder profile. Polymarket can tell these come from Mitoshi users, and when, not which one.
