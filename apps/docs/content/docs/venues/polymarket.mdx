## Set up

Polymarket's official TypeScript SDK runs in its own Web Worker, and every call it makes is sealed with Oblivious
HTTP. The wallet's **Deposit Wallet** is deployed and approved **gasless** through Polymarket's relayer. The Builder
secret that authorises those relayer calls stays on Mitoshi's gateway, which signs **only Deposit Wallet requests**
(`services/gateway/src/builder.ts`).

## Funding

<Steps>
<Step>
One standard amount of ZEC goes to a NEAR Intents deposit address.
</Step>
<Step>
NEAR delivers USDC to the wallet's **Polymarket bridge address**: on Polygon, or on Arbitrum or Base when NEAR has no
Polygon route at that moment. The bridge takes either.
</Step>
<Step>
The bridge credits **pUSD** to the Deposit Wallet, in about 30 seconds.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Trading

- Buy or sell any outcome **at market** (fill-and-kill, with a price guard) or with a **limit**.
- Cancel open orders; **redeem** winning shares after the market resolves.
- Adding to a position or selling it stays on the wallet that holds it. A new market takes a ready, never-used wallet.
- Orders carry **no builder code**: Polymarket publishes the builder field when it is set.

## Coming home

Open orders are cancelled, resolved shares redeemed, the rest sold. The pUSD leaves through a **bridge withdrawal** that
pays a NEAR deposit address in USDC, and NEAR swaps it to ZEC at a fresh shielded address. Under 1 pUSD stays behind as
dust.

<Callout title="Not in NEAR's confidential mode">
This trip home is the one swap Mitoshi cannot quote in NEAR's confidential mode: the bridge pays out whatever arrives,
which needs a flexible-input quote, and 1Click offers no confidential mode for those.
</Callout>

## Public on purpose

Each wallet's **relayer calls** (creating it, its approvals, redeeming, moving pUSD into Perps, the trip home) are
attributed to Mitoshi's builder profile. Polymarket can tell these come from Mitoshi users, and when, not which user.
