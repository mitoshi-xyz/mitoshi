## Set up

Perps trades from the same kind of fresh wallet as [Polymarket](/docs/venues/polymarket). The wallet's own key owns
the Perps account and authorises a **proxy key for 7 days** with an EIP-712 signature; the proxy signs each order. A new
proxy is authorised an hour before the old one expires, or if it is refused. Every request is a signed command over
Polymarket's REST API, so it is sealed like everything else.

## Funding

1. The wallet is funded with pUSD, exactly as on [Polymarket](/docs/venues/polymarket#funding).
2. On its first Perps order, all of its pUSD moves from the Deposit Wallet into Perps, **gasless**. That first deposit,
   at least **10 pUSD**, opens the Perps account.

- **Long or short** at market (an IOC limit at most 1% through the mark) or with a limit (good till cancelled).
- **Leverage** (5× by default), **take-profit** and **stop-loss** exits; see the [ticket's arithmetic](/docs/guide/trade#the-ticket).
- The terminal trades from the wallet that already holds this market, otherwise from a ready wallet that has never
  traded, otherwise it asks you to fund one.

## Coming home

Two steps, as on the venue: first **Close** each position. Then **Send home** cancels any orders, withdraws the pUSD
back to the Deposit Wallet with the owner's signature, and takes [Polymarket's route home](/docs/venues/polymarket#coming-home).
Send home refuses while a position is open. Under 1 pUSD withdrawable stays behind as dust.

## Live data

Best bid and offer, trades, candles and daily statistics come from Polymarket Perps' public feed through the relay's
shared live feed. The
relay skips the `tickers` channel (about 700 updates a second across every market) and uses `statistics` for the
price, at most once a second.
