## Set up

Perps uses the same kind of fresh Polymarket wallet as [Polymarket](/venues/polymarket). The wallet's own key owns
the Perps account and authorises a **proxy key for 7 days**, which signs each order. A new proxy is authorised when one
expires or is lost. Every request is a signed command over Polymarket's REST API, so it goes through Oblivious HTTP
like everything else.

## Funding

1. The wallet is funded with pUSD, as on [Polymarket](/venues/polymarket#funding).
2. Its pUSD moves from the Deposit Wallet into Perps, **gasless**. That first deposit, at least **10 pUSD**, opens the
   Perps account.

## Trading

- **Long or short** at market (an IOC limit at most 1% through the mark) or with a limit.
- **Leverage**, and **take-profit** and **stop-loss** exits.
- The terminal trades from the wallet that already traded this market; otherwise from a ready, unused wallet.

## Coming home

Orders are cancelled and positions closed. The withdrawable pUSD goes back to the Deposit Wallet, signed by the
wallet's own key, and then takes [Polymarket's route home](/venues/polymarket#coming-home). Under 1 pUSD stays behind as
dust.

## Live data

Prices, candles and books come from Polymarket Perps' public feed through the relay's live feed. The relay skips the
`tickers` channel: it pushes about 700 updates a second for every market at once, and `statistics` gives the price.
