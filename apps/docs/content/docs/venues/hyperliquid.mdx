## Funding

NEAR delivers **USDC on HyperCore** straight to the fresh address. Mitoshi moves it from spot to perps when a trade
needs it. A brand-new Hyperliquid account pays Hyperliquid's one-time **1 USDC activation fee**.

## Trading

- Long or short with **leverage**, **take-profit** and **stop-loss**, and close, from the live terminal.
- Orders go through Hyperliquid's API, signed by the wallet's own key, with the official SDK's transport replaced by
  Oblivious HTTP.
- The terminal trades from the wallet that already traded this market; otherwise from a ready, unused wallet.

## Coming home

Positions are closed, the USDC is gathered in spot and sent to a NEAR deposit address, and NEAR swaps it to ZEC at a
fresh shielded address. Under 1 USDC stays behind as dust.

## Live data

Mids, candles, books and trades come from one shared WebSocket the relay keeps open to Hyperliquid. Only market
channels are allowed: anything that names a user is refused, so the relay never learns which address is yours.
