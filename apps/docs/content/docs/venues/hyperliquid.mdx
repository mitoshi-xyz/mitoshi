## Funding

NEAR delivers **USDC on HyperCore** straight to the fresh address. On the first order, Mitoshi moves it from spot to
perps when the account keeps them apart.

## Trading

<Shot src="/shots/wide/picker.webp" alt="The market picker on mitoshi.xyz: Hyperliquid and Polymarket tabs, a search box, categories, and a list of markets with prices." caption="The market picker switches between Hyperliquid and Polymarket Perps." />

- Long or short with **leverage**, **take-profit** and **stop-loss**, from the live terminal.
- Market orders are IOC limits 1% through the mid. Orders go through the `@nktkas/hyperliquid` TypeScript SDK with its transport
  replaced by Oblivious HTTP, signed by the wallet's own key.
- The ticket's fee line uses 0.045% (Hyperliquid's base taker rate); the smallest position is $10.
- The terminal trades from the wallet that already holds this market, otherwise from a ready wallet that has never
  traded, otherwise it asks you to fund one.

## Coming home

Two steps: **Close** each position, then **Send home**: orders are cancelled, perp USDC goes back to spot and is sent
to a NEAR deposit address, and NEAR swaps it to ZEC at a fresh shielded address. Send home refuses while a position is
open. Under 1 USDC stays behind as dust; if NEAR refunds the trip home, the USDC returns to the wallet.

## Live data

Mids, candles, books, trades and asset context come from one WebSocket the relay keeps open to Hyperliquid for
everyone. Only market channels are allowed: anything that names a user is refused, so the relay never learns which
address is yours.
