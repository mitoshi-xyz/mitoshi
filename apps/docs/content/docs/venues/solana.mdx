## Funding

NEAR delivers **SOL** to the fresh address. When buying, the wallet keeps 0.01 SOL back for fees and token-account rent.

## Trading

- Swaps go through **Jupiter Ultra**: Jupiter quotes and routes the order, the wallet signs only its own part of the
  transaction in the browser (it refuses one it isn't asked to sign), and Jupiter broadcasts it.
- Charts and pool data come from GeckoTerminal, prices and lists from Jupiter, all through the gateway.
- A token you already hold trades from the wallet that holds it; a new token takes a ready wallet that has never traded,
  or asks you to fund one.

## Coming home

**Sell all and send home** sells every token back to SOL through Jupiter, then one transfer goes to a NEAR deposit
address and NEAR swaps it to ZEC at a fresh shielded address. Under 0.005 SOL stays behind as dust.
