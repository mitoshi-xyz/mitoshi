## Funding

NEAR delivers **SOL** to the fresh address. The same SOL pays the wallet's transaction fees.

## Trading

- Swaps go through **Jupiter Ultra**: Jupiter routes the swap and broadcasts the transaction this wallet signed.
- One Solana wallet trades until you send it home, so it can hold several tokens. A new one is used when none is
  trading.
- Charts and prices come from Jupiter and GeckoTerminal through the gateway.

## Coming home

Every token is sold back to SOL, then one transfer goes to a NEAR deposit address, and NEAR swaps it to ZEC at a fresh
shielded address. Under 0.005 SOL stays behind as dust.
