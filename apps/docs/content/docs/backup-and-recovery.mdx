Mitoshi keeps nothing for you on a server, so recovery is yours.

| Way back | Restores | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **24 words** | The balance and every private wallet | Slow: a private scan of the chain, then of each venue |
| **Backup file** | Words and the wallet list, with each wallet's funding record | At once, no scan |

## The 24 words

The words restore the shielded balance with a private scan from the Ironwood upgrade, and every private wallet,
because each wallet's key comes from them. After a restore from words alone, Mitoshi asks each venue about one derived
address at a time, 3–9 seconds apart, until it meets five unused ones in a row. **Settings → Find my wallets** runs the
scan again.

## The backup file

**Settings → Download the backup** makes one file with your words and your private-wallet list, locked with a
passphrase you choose (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 iterations, then AES-GCM). Restoring from it brings every wallet back
with no scan.

## The passphrase lock

**Settings → Passphrase lock** asks for a passphrase each time Mitoshi opens in this browser. The vault key is then
kept only wrapped under the passphrase, and a locked vault opens nothing, not even the wallet engine. There is no
reset: if the passphrase is lost, only the 24 words get you back in.

## Starting over

<Steps>
<Step>
**Settings → Forget this browser**, behind a typed confirmation, erases the vault here. Your money stays on the chains.
</Step>
<Step>
Restore from the 24 words or the backup file, in any browser.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
Anyone with your 24 words can move your money. Keep them offline and never type them into a website.
</Callout>

## If something stops halfway

| What stopped | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A funding failed before anything was sent | It says so; Try again waits for a new random start |
| A funding's ZEC was sent | It goes back to following the swap; NEAR refunds to a fresh shielded address if it cannot complete |
| NEAR refunded a trip home | The money is back in the venue wallet, ready to send home again |
| The tab closed while a wallet was being set up | It finishes the next time Mitoshi opens |
