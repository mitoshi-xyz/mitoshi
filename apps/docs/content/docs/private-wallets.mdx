A private wallet is a venue account made from a **new key**, funded once and sent home once. No two share an
address, a key or a swap.

## What makes one

- **Its own key**, from your 24 words on a hardened path of Mitoshi's own: `m/44'/coin'/6874'/venue'/index'`
  (coin 60 for Polymarket and Hyperliquid, 501 for Solana). 6874 spells MTSH on a phone keypad, so the path never
  collides with a normal wallet's.
- **A standard size**: 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 ZEC, so its one public hop looks like many others.
- **Only on this device.** The list of your wallets lives in the encrypted vault in your browser; no server holds it.

## Which wallet an order uses

| Venue | The wallet |
| --- | --- |
| Polymarket | One market per wallet: the wallet that holds it, otherwise a ready, never-used one |
| Hyperliquid, Polymarket Perps | The wallet that already traded this market, otherwise a ready, unused one; with none ready, the wallet already trading |
| Solana | The wallet already trading (it can hold several tokens until you send it home), otherwise a ready one |

With **Keep one ready** on (below), a new position finds a fresh wallet waiting.

## Its life

<Lifecycle />

| Step | What happens | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preparing** | A key is derived; funding waits for a random moment | 20 s to 3 min |
| **Funding** | A signed 1Click quote, then one shielded payment of exactly the wallet's size | About 7.5 min |
| **Ready** | Funded and unused | Until you trade |
| **Trading** | Every order signed by this wallet's key | Until you send it home |
| **Coming home** | Positions closed, the rest swapped to ZEC at a fresh shielded address | About 2.5 min, then a 2–6 h rest |
| **Retired** | Empty; it is never used again | — |

A funding NEAR could not complete is **refunded** to a fresh shielded address. A step that **failed** before anything
was sent can be tried again; it waits for a new random start.

## Keep one ready

A trade waits for a funding unless a wallet is already funded. **Keep one ready** holds one funded wallet per venue,
at a size you pick, while Mitoshi is open.

When the ready wallet is used, its replacement does **not** start right away. It waits a random time drawn from an
exponential spread: **90 minutes on average, never more than 12 hours** (`refillTime` in
`packages/engine/src/schedule.ts`). So a new wallet never shows up on the venue minutes after your trade, and two of
your wallets cannot be chained by timing.

A funding that came due while the app was closed gets a new random start, instead of going out the moment you open
Mitoshi.

## Dust

Coming home costs a swap. Whatever is smaller than the trip home is **left in the venue wallet as dust**, never merged
into another wallet:

| Venue | Left as dust |
| --- | --- |
| Polymarket, Polymarket Perps | Under 1 pUSD |
| Hyperliquid | Under 1 USDC |
| Solana | Under 0.005 SOL |

## What the public sees

Each wallet has a **What the public sees** view: its public record as a copy trader would read it, fetched through the
relay. It shows the orders filled, its one deposit and one withdrawal, open positions, no history before it, and no
links to your other wallets.
