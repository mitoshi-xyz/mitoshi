# Venue marks

Vendored once so the app never loads a mark from a third party at runtime.

- `polymarket.svg`: the vector of Polymarket's mark from `https://polymarket.com/icons/safari-pinned-tab.svg` (its pinned-tab icon, colour `#1652f0`), filled white on that blue.
- `jupiter.svg`: `https://jup.ag/svg/jupiter-logo.svg`.
- Hyperliquid, Solana, NEAR and Zcash come from `@web3icons/core` (MIT) when `scripts/marks.mjs` runs.

The marks belong to their owners. They are used only to name each venue.
